Time and filters can be combined, for example “10:15 to 10:20, only course 17” (example A8 in the file):

start_time: "2026-11-17T10:15:00.000Z"
end_time:   "2026-11-17T10:20:00.000Z"
filters { column: "course" op: "=" value: "17" }

How the three ways of asking compare:

You give	You get
Only start_time / end_time	Everything in that time window
Only filters (run, course, tow)	That run, course or tow, at any time
Both	Only the part that matches both
