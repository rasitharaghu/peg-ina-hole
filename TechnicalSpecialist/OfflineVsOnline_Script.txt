Imagine a new aircraft panel arrives with a different shape or position. The inspection task is familiar, but the robot’s movement needs to change.

For stable, repeated parts, offline programming works well. As variation increases, engineers may repeatedly adjust paths, download programs and run trials on the robot.

Autonomous motion helps reduce that repeated effort. It generates movement from the part model and scan, checks reach and collision risks, and adapts to the measured part position.

This can mean faster preparation for new variants, fewer physical trials and less manual correction. Offline preparation and online adaptation can also work together.

For Airbus, this means less reprogramming when panels change, freeing engineering time to improve process quality.
