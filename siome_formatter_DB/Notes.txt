One note: if you want the SIOME5 parent-child hierarchy to remain completely untouched, comment out this block in _update_metadata_from_db():

if db_parent_node_id:
    variable.set("ParentNodeId", str(db_parent_node_id))
	
	
Your views.py can remain the same:

xml_data = generate_siome5_xml_from_db_nodes(
    nodes=nodes,
    template_path=str(template_path),
)

This will give:

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="utf-8"?>
<UANodeSet ... LastModified="current-date-time">

and it will avoid adding new <Value> tags where the SIOME template did not have them.
